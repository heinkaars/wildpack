# **Core loop funnel**  

### **Core function**

Build a lifelist by photographing wildlife, and learn facts and earn wins along the way.

### **Core loop**

Photograph wildlife → Identify wildlife → Celebrate → Add wildlife to lifelist → Ask questions about that species 

### **Accessory features**

Camera  
Lifelist  
Streaks   
Milestone   
Celebration modal   
Species card  
AMA modal (per-species: ID help \+ species facts only)

### **Surface area**

Lifelist (2 screens)

- Profile \+ lifelist \+ achievements  
- Species card \+ AMA modal (re-usable in capture flow) 

Capture (3 screens) 

- Capture \+ Identify \+ Result   
- Celebration modal   
- Species card \+ AMA modal (re-usable in lifelist flow) 

### **Retention hook** 

Streaks  
Milestones
